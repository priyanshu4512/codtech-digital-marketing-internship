YouTube Channel Strategy – AMA Cafe

1. Channel Overview

Channel: AMA Cafe
Industry: Cafe / Food & Beverage
Primary Location: Dehradun
Target Audience: Food lovers, coffee lovers, students, young professionals and tourists.

Channel Goal

The main goal is to use YouTube to increase AMA Cafe's brand awareness and attract people searching for cafes, coffee and food experiences in Dehradun.

---

2. Channel Positioning

The channel should focus on:

Coffee + Food + Cafe Experience + Dehradun

Instead of making only promotional videos, the content should show why people would want to visit AMA Cafe.

---

3. Target Audience

Audience| Content Interest
Students| Affordable food, cafe atmosphere, study/work space
Young professionals| Coffee, breakfast and relaxing environment
Tourists| Dehradun cafe experiences
Coffee lovers| Coffee preparation and specialty coffee
Food lovers| Breakfast, bakery and desserts

---

4. Content Pillars

1. Cafe Experience

Videos showing the atmosphere, interior and overall experience.

2. Food & Coffee

Show coffee, breakfast, bakery items and popular dishes.

3. Behind the Scenes

Coffee preparation, baking and staff activities.

4. Dehradun Content

Videos connected to local places, food and weekend activities.

5. Customer Experience

Customer reviews, reactions and suitable user-generated content.

---

5. Video Ideas

1. A Morning at AMA Cafe Dehradun
2. What to Order at AMA Cafe?
3. Inside AMA Cafe Rajpur Road
4. How AMA Cafe Makes Its Coffee
5. Best Breakfast Options at AMA Cafe
6. A Day at AMA Cafe
7. Coffee + Breakfast in Dehradun
8. POV: You Found a Peaceful Cafe in Dehradun
9. AMA Cafe Customer Favourites
10. 5 Things to Try at AMA Cafe

---

6. Upload Strategy

I would keep the schedule realistic rather than uploading every day.

Weekly plan:

- 1 long video: 4–8 minutes
- 2 Shorts: 15–60 seconds
- 2–3 Community posts: polls, questions or photos

Shorts can be used to attract new viewers, while longer videos can provide more detailed information about the cafe.

---

7. YouTube SEO

Each video should include:

Title

Use the main topic + location.

Example:

Best Cafe in Dehradun? | AMA Cafe Rajpur Road

Description

Include:

- Short video summary
- AMA Cafe location
- Website
- Relevant keywords
- Social-media links

Keywords

Examples:

- AMA Cafe Dehradun
- cafe in Dehradun
- Rajpur Road cafe
- best cafe Dehradun
- coffee Dehradun
- breakfast cafe Dehradun

---

8. Thumbnail Strategy

Thumbnails should be simple and easy to understand.

Use:

- One strong food/coffee image
- Short text of 2–4 words
- Clear subject
- Consistent brand style

Example thumbnail text:

“BEST BREAKFAST?”

or

“AMA CAFE DEHRADUN”

Avoid putting too much text on thumbnails.

---

9. Engagement Strategy

The channel should encourage viewers to interact.

Examples:

- Ask viewers what they would order.
- Ask for their favourite coffee.
- Reply to comments.
- Use Community polls.
- Feature suitable customer content.
- Encourage viewers to visit the cafe and share their experience.

---

10. Key Performance Indicators

The channel should track:

- Views
- Watch time
- Average view duration
- Audience retention
- Click-through rate
- Subscribers
- Likes, comments and shares
- Website clicks
- Traffic from YouTube to the business

---

11. Conclusion

AMA Cafe can use YouTube as more than an advertising platform. The strongest strategy is to combine food, coffee, cafe experiences and Dehradun-focused content.

Shorts can help reach new viewers, while longer videos can build stronger interest in the cafe. The main objective should be turning online attention into local awareness and actual visits.
