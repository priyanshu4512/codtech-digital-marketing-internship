SEO Audit of AMA Cafe – Rajpur Road, Dehradun

1. Business Overview

Business: AMA Cafe
Location: 143, Rajpur Road, Kishanpur, Dehradun, Uttarakhand 248001
Industry: Cafe / Food & Beverage
Website: https://amacafe.co/

AMA Cafe is a Himalayan-inspired cafe brand with outlets in Delhi and Dehradun. The Rajpur Road outlet offers coffee, breakfast, bakery products, desserts and other cafe items.

Audit Objective: To evaluate AMA Cafe's website, on-page SEO, technical SEO, local SEO and keyword opportunities and provide practical recommendations.

3. On-Page SEO

Strengths

- Google indexes multiple AMA Cafe pages.
- Page titles include relevant location and service terms.
- The website contains original brand-story content.
- Dehradun-focused content is already present.
- Products and services such as coffee and breakfast are clearly presented.

Issues

The homepage's primary visible heading focuses mainly on "Coffee". This is good for branding but weak for local SEO because it does not clearly communicate the Dehradun/Rajpur Road location.

The mobile navigation uses broad labels such as "AMA", "SHOP" and "INFO", which could be more descriptive.

Recommendations

Use headings and navigation such as:

- AMA Cafe Rajpur Road, Dehradun
- Menu
- Locations
- About AMA Cafe
- Contact

Recommended H1 for the Rajpur Road location page:

AMA Cafe – Rajpur Road, Dehradun

5. Local SEO

Local SEO is one of AMA Cafe's strongest areas.

The Rajpur Road business listing shows:

- Rating: Approximately 4.8/5
- Reviews: 9,000+
- Address: 143, Rajpur Road, Kishanpur, Dehradun
- Category: Cafe
- Opening hours: 8:00 AM – 10:30 PM

The website also clearly provides location and contact information.

Recommendations

1. Keep Google Business Profile information accurate.
2. Continue collecting genuine customer reviews.
3. Respond to customer reviews regularly.
4. Upload fresh, high-quality cafe and food photos.
5. Maintain consistent Name, Address and Phone information across online directories.
6. Create/optimize a dedicated Rajpur Road, Dehradun location page.

10. Conclusion

AMA Cafe has a strong foundation for local SEO because of its established brand, Google presence, large review base, website and existing Dehradun-focused content.

The main opportunity is to make its Rajpur Road and Dehradun search intent clearer through stronger location pages, headings, keywords, internal linking and local content.

Overall SEO Assessment

Good foundation — significant opportunity for stronger local search visibility.
