Social Media Strategy Plan – AMA Cafe

1. Introduction

AMA Cafe is a cafe brand with locations in Delhi and Dehradun. The purpose of this social media strategy is to improve its local visibility, attract new customers and increase engagement through Instagram and Facebook.

Target Location: Dehradun
Target Audience: College students, young professionals, tourists, coffee lovers and people looking for cafes to work or relax.

---

2. Social Media Objectives

The main objectives are:

- Increase local awareness of AMA Cafe.
- Bring more people to the Rajpur Road outlet.
- Increase Instagram and Facebook engagement.
- Promote coffee, breakfast, bakery items and the cafe atmosphere.
- Encourage customers to share their own photos and experiences.

---

3. Target Audience

Audience| What they may look for
College students| Affordable food, relaxing place, Wi-Fi/work space
Young professionals| Coffee, breakfast and work-friendly environment
Tourists| Popular Dehradun cafe and local experience
Coffee lovers| Specialty coffee and cafe atmosphere
Food lovers| Breakfast, bakery and desserts

---

4. Recommended Platforms

Instagram

Main platform for visual content.

Content: Reels, food photos, Stories, customer content and behind-the-scenes videos.

Facebook

Useful for local customers and community reach.

Content: Offers, events, photos, reviews and important announcements.

Google Business Profile

Important for local discovery.

Content: Fresh photos, updates, offers and customer reviews.

---

5. Content Strategy

I would use five main content categories:

1. Food & Coffee – Coffee, breakfast, bakery and desserts.
2. Cafe Experience – Interior, atmosphere and customer experience.
3. Behind the Scenes – Coffee preparation, baking and staff activities.
4. Local Content – Dehradun and Rajpur Road-related posts.
5. Customer Content – Reposting genuine customer photos and reviews.

---

6. Weekly Posting Plan

Day| Content
Monday| Coffee/food Reel
Tuesday| Instagram Story – Poll or Question
Wednesday| Cafe atmosphere/photo
Thursday| Behind-the-scenes Reel
Friday| Breakfast/food post
Saturday| Customer photo/review
Sunday| Weekend Reel or Story

Around 4–5 main posts/reels per week would be enough, supported by regular Stories.

---

7. Example Content Ideas

- “A morning at AMA Cafe”
- “What to order at AMA Cafe?”
- Coffee-making behind the scenes
- Best breakfast options
- A relaxing evening at Rajpur Road
- Customer review Reel
- “POV: You found your new favourite cafe”
- Short video showing the cafe atmosphere
- Dehradun weekend cafe recommendation

---

8. Hashtag Strategy

Use a mix of brand, location and category hashtags.

Example:

"#AMACafe #DehradunCafe #DehradunFood #RajpurRoad #DehradunFoodies #CoffeeLovers #CafeInDehradun #Dehradun"

Hashtags should support the content rather than relying on a large number of unrelated hashtags.

---

9. Engagement Strategy

AMA Cafe should:

- Reply to comments and messages.
- Encourage customers to tag the cafe.
- Repost suitable customer-generated content.
- Use polls and questions in Stories.
- Respond to reviews professionally.
- Collaborate with relevant local food creators.

The focus should be on local engagement, not just increasing follower count.

---

10. Key Performance Indicators

The strategy can be measured using:

- Reach
- Reel views
- Engagement rate
- Saves and shares
- Profile visits
- Website clicks
- Messages
- Customer-generated posts
- Google Business Profile actions

---

11. Conclusion

AMA Cafe already has a visually strong product and a location that can work well on social media. The main strategy should be to show the food, coffee and overall cafe experience, while keeping the content strongly connected to Dehradun.

A consistent mix of Reels, Stories, customer content and local posts can help AMA Cafe improve awareness and attract more customers to its Rajpur Road outlet.
